# Controlled data and repository contribution policy

This is the authors' fixed publication repository for [arXiv:2610.16220](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16220). The official analysis is maintained by authorized authors, and outside code contributions or pull requests are not accepted.

Source clinical and gait CSVs must be obtained from [Synapse syn52540892](https://doi.org/10.7303/syn52540892) under the provider's access terms. Runtime data, participant-level records, raw predictions and clinical intermediates must remain private.

No blanket license for third-party modification or redistribution is granted by this repository. GitHub's platform terms and applicable legal exceptions still apply. Contact the authors about additional permissions or reproducibility questions.
